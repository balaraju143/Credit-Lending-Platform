|| FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ||

Everything you need to know
about credit & lending

Find clear answers to common questions about loans, credit, eligibility and the lending process.

Stackly provides guidance across personal, home, business, education and other lending options based on your financial requirements.

A secured loan generally involves an asset as security, while an unsecured loan does not require the same type of collateral. The right option depends on your circumstances and lender requirements.

Eligibility can depend on factors such as income, credit history, existing financial commitments, repayment capacity and the requirements of the selected lending option.

Interest rates may vary according to the loan type, credit profile, repayment period, financial circumstances and the terms offered by the lender.

The process generally starts by understanding your requirements, reviewing eligibility and submitting the required information before the lender evaluates the application.
